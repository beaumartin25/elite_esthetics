Our Story

Why Troy Martin Founded
Elite Esthetics Academy

“I started Elite Esthetics Academy because I saw talented students graduating with licenses but still feeling unprepared for the realities of the industry. I wanted to create a school that combines exceptional technical training with mentorship, confidence-building, and business education so graduates leave ready to build meaningful, successful careers — not just pass a test.”

Troy Martin
Founder & CEO, Elite Esthetics Academy

Our Vision for Esthetics Education

The ideal esthetics school does more than help students earn a license — it prepares them for a successful, long-term career. It combines expert instruction, hands-on experience, mentorship, and real-world business education in an environment where students feel supported, challenged, and inspired to grow.

At Elite Esthetics Academy, we’ve built our program around that vision. We believe students learn best through hands-on practice, personalized guidance, and exposure to the latest industry techniques and technology. That’s why our curriculum goes beyond state requirements to provide real-world training that helps students build confidence, refine their skills, and develop a professional mindset.

Elite Esthetics Academy Today

Today, Elite Esthetics Academy is more than a school — it’s a community dedicated to developing the next generation of confident, skilled estheticians. What we’re most proud of is the success of our students and graduates who leave with not only a license, but the knowledge, confidence, and passion to build meaningful careers in the beauty industry.

We’ve created an environment where education is personal, hands-on, and focused on real-world success. When students walk through our doors, we want them to feel welcomed, supported, and inspired by what’s possible for their future. Most importantly, we want every student to know they belong here and that we are fully invested in helping them achieve their goals.
